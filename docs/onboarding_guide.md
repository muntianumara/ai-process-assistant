# Onboarding Guide for New Employees

| Document ID | Version | Effective date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR-GUI-002 | 1.4 | 15 February 2026 | HR Onboarding Team, Contoso Business Services |

## 1. Purpose

This guide describes what happens before your first day, during your first week and during
your first 90 days at Contoso Business Services (CBS), and who can help you at each stage.

## 2. Before your first day

1. About **5 working days** before your start date you receive a welcome e-mail from
   `onboarding@contoso-bs.example` with your start time and your login details for the HR Portal.
2. Sign your employment contract digitally in the HR Portal.
3. Upload a copy of your ID card and your bank account details in the HR Portal at least
   **3 working days** before your first day.

## 3. Your first day

| Time | Activity | Where / Who |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Arrival and access badge (bring your ID card) | Reception, Building B, ground floor |
| 09:30 | Welcome session | HR Onboarding Team, meeting room B-105 |
| 10:30 | Laptop handover, password setup and multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Microsoft Authenticator app | IT Service Desk, room B-214 |
| 12:00 | Lunch with your team | Your line manager |
| 13:30 | Meeting with your **buddy** | Your buddy |

Your **buddy** is an experienced colleague from your team who answers your practical questions
during your first **3 months**.

If your laptop or accounts do not work after the first day, report it as an incident according
to the *IT Incident Reporting Procedure (IT-PRO-012)*.

## 4. Your first week

- Complete the mandatory online trainings in the Learning Portal within your first
  **5 working days**:
  - Information Security Basics (60 minutes);
  - GDPR and Data Protection (45 minutes);
  - Health and Safety at Work (30 minutes).
- Read the Code of Conduct and confirm it in the HR Portal.
- Have a **30-minute goals meeting** with your line manager to agree on your objectives for the
  probation period.

## 5. Your first 90 days (probation period)

- The probation period lasts **90 calendar days**.
- Check-in meetings with your line manager take place after **30**, **60** and **90** days.
- At the 90-day meeting, your manager confirms the end of your probation period.
- During the probation period you may take **up to 5 days** of annual leave, with the approval of
  your line manager. Your total annual leave entitlement is calculated according to the
  *Leave Policy (HR-POL-004)*.
- Remote work during the probation period is regulated by the *Hybrid Work Policy (HR-POL-010)*.

## 6. Who does what

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Line manager | Sets your objectives, approves your leave and access rights, runs the check-in meetings |
| Buddy | Answers practical day-to-day questions during your first 3 months |
| HR Onboarding Team | Contract, documents, mandatory trainings, welcome session |
| IT Service Desk | Laptop, accounts, passwords, MFA; technical problems are handled as incidents (IT-PRO-012) |

Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00.
