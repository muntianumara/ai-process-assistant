# Leave Policy

| Document ID | Version | Effective date | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR-POL-004 | 2.1 | 1 January 2026 | HR Department, Contoso Business Services |

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how employees of Contoso Business Services (CBS) request and use paid leave.
It applies to all permanent and fixed-term employees. Interns and external contractors are
**not** covered by this policy; their leave is defined in their individual contracts.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

- Full-time employees receive **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours
  (for example, an employee working 20 hours per week receives 12 days).
- After **5 years** of continuous service, employees receive **1 extra day** for every additional
  5 years of service, up to a maximum of **27 days** per year.
- Employees who join during the year receive a pro-rata entitlement from their start date,
  rounded up to the nearest half day.

## 3. How to request annual leave

1. Submit the request in the **HR Portal** (`https://hrportal.contoso-bs.example`) under *My Time Off*.
2. Respect the minimum notice period:
   - leave of **3 or more consecutive working days**: at least **10 working days** in advance;
   - leave of 1–2 days: at least **2 working days** in advance.
3. Your line manager approves or rejects the request within **3 working days**.
   If the manager does not respond in time, the request is **automatically escalated** to the
   department head.
4. Leave longer than **15 consecutive working days** also requires approval from the department head.

## 4. Carry-over of unused leave

- A maximum of **5 unused days** can be carried over to the next calendar year.
- Carried-over days must be used by **31 March**; after this date they are lost.
- Unused leave is not paid out, except when the employment contract ends.

## 5. Sick leave

1. Inform your line manager and the HR Service Desk **before 10:00** on the first day of absence.
2. A **medical certificate** is required for absences longer than **2 consecutive working days**.
3. Upload the medical certificate in the HR Portal within **5 working days** after you return to work.

## 6. Special paid leave

| Event | Paid days |
|---|---|
| Your own wedding | 5 |
| Birth or adoption of a child | 10 |
| Death of a close family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) | 3 |
| Moving to a new home (once per calendar year) | 1 |

Special leave is requested in the HR Portal under *Special Leave* and must be supported by a
document (for example, a marriage or birth certificate) within **30 days**.

Maternity leave and parental leave are regulated by national law and described in a separate
document, the *Parental Leave Policy (HR-POL-007)*.

## 7. Public holidays

Public holidays are not deducted from annual leave. The public holiday calendar for the next
year is published in the HR Portal every December.
